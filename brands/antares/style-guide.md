# Antares — video style guide

Source: https://getantares.io/brandkit/ (read 2026-11-03). Rules: https://getantares.io/brand/agents.md.

Use one blue only, sentence-case two-beat headlines, flat surfaces, no gradients or shadows. Use the drawn wordmark alone, not mark plus wordmark; minimum width 120px and clear space half artwork height. Helvetica system fallback is intentional; do not bundle substitute webfonts. Never quote effort, speed or savings percentages, customer names or proof points. Marketing branding does not specify application UI.

## Toolkit scope

The two universal templates are neutral editorial layouts, not reconstructed
application UI. Disclosures are always visible, including when `demo` is false.
Use an authorized real capture for a product walkthrough; do not claim these
layouts are a working product screen. Do not invent metrics, customer evidence,
savings, pricing or model availability. The example JSON makes no product claims.

Output language is exactly `en`. Semantically review authored text and actual
speech for English; character checks and audio timing do not detect language.
Voice is not preselected: `voice: null` requires explicit intake.
Shorts geometry and reference mask are owned by `formats/shorts/`, not the brand.
Inspect actual frames for text fit, contrast, logo space and final-resolution size.

## Assets and rights

See `assets/provenance.json`; bundled fonts retain their OFL texts. Official logo
artwork is unmodified and is used only to refer to its owner. No third-party
platform logos or LakeSentry media are copied. Do not infer blanket trademark rights.
